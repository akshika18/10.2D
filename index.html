DOSE WINDOWS

How deadlines work

Morning

Scans are accepted from the morning start time through the morning deadline.

Evening

Scans are accepted from the evening start time through the evening deadline.

After the deadline

The system sends an overdue alert and rejects late automatic dispensing. Follow the clinician's late-dose instructions.

Duplicate protection

Only one dose event per patient, slot and local calendar day can be authorized.
